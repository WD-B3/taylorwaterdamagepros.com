What to have ready when you call

  1. Your address and the nearest cross street.
  2. Where the water is coming from, if you know.
  3. Whether the water has been shut off.
  4. Which rooms are wet and roughly how much.
  5. Your insurance company, if you have it handy.

None of this is required. If you do not know an answer, say so and the dispatcher will walk you through what to check. If you smell gas, see water touching a panel or hear electrical noise, leave and call 911 first.

While you wait for the crew

  • Shut off the water at the main if you can reach it safely.
  • Keep people and pets out of standing water.
  • Take photos or video of the damage before anything is moved.
  • Lift small items and furniture off wet floors.
  • Do not run a household vacuum on wet carpet.

For longer guidance, see the water extraction page and the burst pipe page. To learn about the towns we cover, visit the service area page.
