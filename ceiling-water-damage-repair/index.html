Emergency Ceiling Water Damage Repair in Taylor, MI

A stained, bulging or dripping ceiling means water is trapped above it. Call the 24/7 line before the drywall lets go.

☎ (734) 865-2522

Ceiling water damage repair in Taylor, MI begins with a hard fact: a ceiling stain is the end of a trip, not the start.

Water travels along joists and across the back of drywall before it shows itself, so the wet area is nearly always larger than the stain. If the ceiling is bulging or dripping, treat it as an emergency, keep people out from under it and call our 24/7 dispatch line. A saturated ceiling can come down without warning.

This page owns the finished ceiling and everything above it, whatever the source. The cause may be a roof leak, an upstairs bathroom, a supply line or condensation in an attic. The crew traces it, dries the cavity and repairs the surface.

When a ceiling leak is an emergency

Act at once if the drywall is sagging, water is dripping, light fixtures are wet or the stain is spreading. Do not poke a bulge with your hand, since the water behind it falls on you.

A small bucket and a pin hole at the lowest point of the bulge let the water drain in a controlled way, but only if you are not near a fixture. After-hours calls are normal for ceilings, and same-day response is offered when crews are available.

  • Move furniture and electronics out of the drip zone and cover what cannot move.
  • Turn off power to the room if a light fixture or fan is wet and the panel is safely reachable.
  • Photograph the stain, the bulge and any visible source before touching anything.
  • If water is coming from upstairs, shut off the water to that bathroom or unit.
  • Call a roofer or plumber for the source repair, and call us to dry and restore.

Roof leaks that come through the ceiling

Roof leaks have no page of their own on this site because they end the same way, with water in a ceiling. Rain finds a gap at a flashing, a vent boot, a missing shingle or a failing valley, runs down the roof deck and soaks the insulation above the drywall. Michigan winters add ice dams, where melting snow refreezes at the eave and backs water up under shingles.

The roofer repairs the opening, and the restoration crew handles what the water already did. Wet attic insulation holds moisture against the framing for weeks, so it is usually removed and replaced. The structural drying plan is on the structural drying page, and storm-driven rain that came through walls and windows is on the storm water damage page.

How the repair works

The crew finds the extent with a moisture meter and thermal camera, then cuts out ceiling drywall that is soaked or sagging. Dry sections can sometimes stay. Insulation above is replaced if wet. Air movers and dehumidifiers dry the framing and the cavity, and once readings reach the dry standard the drywall is hung, finished, primed and painted. Textured ceilings are matched as closely as the texture allows.

Moisture in a ceiling can also reach walls through the top plate, which is why the wall and drywall page is the next stop if the damage runs downward. Water that fell through to the floor below is covered on the carpet and hardwood floor pages.

Ceiling repair cost factors

Price follows the size of the area, the source, the access to the attic, the number of rooms below, the drying days and the finish work. Scope and price are explained before work starts. A single stain that is dried early costs far less than a collapsed section, which is another reason not to wait.

Michigan regulators say sudden and accidental water damage is often covered by a homeowners policy, and that gradual leaks and neglect often are not. Roof leaks have their own conditions in many policies, such as age of the roof, so read your policy and ask your insurer. Crews serve Taylor at 48180 and neighboring areas such as Garden City.

Questions people ask

Do you handle ceiling water damage emergencies at night or on weekends in Taylor?

Yes. The line is answered 24 hours a day, every day. A bulging ceiling can fail suddenly, so after-hours calls are handled with the same urgency, and same-day response is offered when crews are available.

Can I just paint over a ceiling stain?

Not safely. Paint over a wet or damp ceiling hides the problem and traps moisture. The source must be fixed and the area dried first.

Do I need to replace the whole ceiling?

Usually not. Only soaked, sagging or contaminated sections are removed. Moisture readings decide how much can stay.

Who fixes the roof?

A roofer repairs the roof. We dry and restore the interior damage and can coordinate timing so the roof is sealed first.

How do I know if the leak is still active?

A damp stain, a drip after rain or a reading on a moisture meter means the area is wet. A dry, stable stain may be old, but have it checked, since it can reactivate.
