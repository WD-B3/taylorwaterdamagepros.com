Emergency Basement Water Damage Cleanup in Taylor, MI

Sump failures, seepage and drain backups flood basements fast. Stay off wet floors near power and call the 24/7 line.

☎ (734) 865-2522

Basement water damage cleanup in Taylor, MI is urgent because a basement is the lowest room in the house and everything drains toward it. Water arrives through floor drains, a failed sump pump, cracks and wall joints or a supply line. Before you go downstairs, think about the electrical panel and the furnace. If water is near either, stay upstairs and call the 24/7 dispatch line and the utility or 911 for any electrical risk.

This page is the below-grade room. The groundwater and creek-flooding side is on the flood damage page, a dirty backup is on the sewage backup page, and the void under a crawl space house is on the crawl space page. Many basement calls turn out to be a mix, and the crew sorts the source on site.

When a wet basement is an emergency

Treat it as urgent when water is standing, rising or near anything electrical, or when a finished room has taken on water. After-hours calls are routine, since basements flood at night during storms. Same-day response is offered when crews are available.

  • Do not go down if water is above outlets or near the panel or furnace.
  • Turn off the breaker for the basement only if you can reach the panel while dry.
  • Check the sump pump and its power. A tripped breaker is a common cause.
  • Lift stored items and electronics off the floor if it is safe to do so.
  • If the water is dark and smells foul, treat it as sewage and stay out.

The sump pump and what it does

A sump pump moves water from a pit in the floor away from the house. It fails for ordinary reasons: a power outage, a stuck float, a clogged intake or a pump that wore out. A battery backup keeps it running through an outage, and a high-water alarm tells you before the pit overflows.

The National Weather Service reported that 4 to 6 inches of rain fell over about four hours across Wayne County on August 11, 2014, which is the kind of event that tests a pump. Test yours before the wet season by pouring water into the pit.

Finished basements and contents

Finished basements have drywall, insulation, carpet and furniture, all of which wick and hold water. The crew cuts drywall above the wet line, removes wet insulation and pad and dries the concrete and framing. Contents are sorted into what can be cleaned, what can be dried and what is discarded. Details on equipment are on the structural drying page.

Carpet and pad on a concrete slab are covered on the carpet page. A wet water heater in the basement is handled on the water heater page. We do not do waterproofing. Restoration puts the room back, and a drainage contractor addresses why the water came in.

Taylor and the FEMA map

To check whether your address sits in a mapped flood zone, use the FEMA Flood Map Service Center, and ask the City of Taylor building department about the rules for rebuilding a basement after water damage. FEMA says you do not have to be in a mapped flood zone to experience a flood.

Basement cost factors

Price follows the depth and area of water, the water category, the finished materials, the drying days and the rebuild. An unfinished basement costs far less than a finished one. Scope and price are explained before work starts.

Michigan's insurance regulator says a sewer backup or failing sump pump may be covered under a homeowners policy, that backup endorsements are limited and that flood is not covered by a standard policy. Ask your insurer which applies, and do not label the loss a flood on the first call. Crews serve Garden City and Inkster basements.

Questions people ask

Do you handle basement flooding emergencies at night or on weekends in Taylor?

Yes. The dispatch line is open around the clock. Storm-related basement floods peak at night, and same-day response is offered when crews are available.

Is it safe to go into a flooded basement?

Not if water is near the panel, the furnace or outlets. Stay upstairs and call. Contaminated water adds another reason to stay out.

How do I know if my sump pump failed?

Look for water in the pit above the float, a pump that does not start, or a tripped breaker. A high-water alarm helps.

Does homeowners insurance cover a basement flood?

A standard policy does not cover flood. A sump failure or sewer backup may be covered, and backup endorsements are limited. Ask your insurer.

Can finished basement drywall be saved?

If the water was clean and the drywall is dried quickly, sometimes. Contaminated water or long exposure usually means cutting it out.
