Carpet Water Damage Restoration in Taylor, MI

Soaked carpet and pad can sometimes be saved and sometimes cannot. The water category and the clock decide. Dispatch is 24/7.

☎ (734) 865-2522

Carpet water damage restoration in Taylor, MI starts with a decision about the water, not the carpet. Clean water from a supply line, caught within hours, can often be extracted and dried in place. Dirty water from a drain, a backup or outside flooding cannot, and the carpet and pad are discarded. The crew checks the source, the time and the readings, then tells you plainly what can be saved.

This page owns carpet and pad. Wood flooring is on the hardwood floor page, and the first removal of standing water is on the water extraction page. Wall-to-wall carpet sits on a pad and a subfloor, and all three layers matter.

The three layers: carpet, pad and subfloor

The carpet is the surface you see, and it dries relatively quickly. The pad underneath is a sponge that holds a lot of water and dries slowly. The subfloor is wood or concrete, and it holds the moisture the longest. Drying carpet without checking the pad and the subfloor is how people end up with a floor that feels dry and smells musty a week later.

When carpet can be saved

  • The water came from a clean supply source, such as a washer hose or a pipe.
  • The carpet was extracted within a day or two of getting wet.
  • The pad can be lifted or dried and the subfloor reads dry.
  • The carpet is not loosely backed or delaminating, and it has no odors.
  • The affected area is not so large that replacement costs less than drying.

If any of those fail, replacement is usually the honest answer. A carpet that smells after drying is a sign that water or contamination is still in the pad. A water category above 1 means the carpet is generally not saved, and the details of those categories are on the sewage backup page and the flood damage page.

How the restoration is done

A weighted extraction wand pulls water out of the carpet and pad. The crew then lifts a corner of the carpet, pulls the pad if needed and sets air movers and dehumidifiers to dry the subfloor and the back of the carpet. Daily moisture readings guide the process, which follows the approach on the structural drying page.

Carpet is generally re-stretched and reinstalled over new pad once the subfloor reads dry. The room is cleaned and deodorized, and baseboards are reinstalled and painted. If the carpet cannot be saved, replacement is part of the scope, and matching a long-discontinued carpet may mean replacing a whole room.

Carpet cost factors

Price follows the area, the carpet and pad type, the water category, the drying days and whether the carpet is saved or replaced. Replacement of a small area can cost more than drying it, but only if the carpet is in good shape. Scope and price are explained before work starts.

To support a claim, take photos or video of the wet carpet before it is lifted, record the date and time and keep the damaged carpet and pad until the insurer says otherwise, which is the state regulator's advice. Sudden, accidental leaks are often covered. Crews serve 48180, Southgate and Riverview.

Questions people ask

Can wet carpet be dried, or does it always have to be replaced?

Clean-water carpet caught early can often be dried. Carpet exposed to dirty water, or left wet for days, usually cannot.

Why does the pad matter so much?

It holds the most water, dries slowest and can keep the subfloor wet. It is also inexpensive, so it is often replaced even when the carpet is saved.

How do I stop a musty smell after drying?

The smell usually means moisture remains in the pad or subfloor. Call and have readings taken. A deodorizer alone will not fix a wet layer.

Do you handle carpet water damage at night or on weekends?

The dispatch line is open 24 hours a day, every day, and same-day response is offered when crews are available.

Will insurance cover replacing carpet?

Often, when the loss was sudden and accidental. Ask your insurer what applies to your policy.
