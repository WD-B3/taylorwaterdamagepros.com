Emergency Frozen Pipe Water Damage in Taylor, MI

A frozen line often shows its crack only when the ice melts. When the water starts, call the 24/7 line and shut off the main.

☎ (734) 865-2522

Frozen pipe water damage in Taylor, MI usually arrives in two stages. First the water inside a line freezes and expands, cracking the pipe or a fitting without any visible leak. Then the temperature rises, the ice melts and the crack opens to a full-pressure flow. That second stage is the emergency. Shut the main valve, open a faucet to drain the line and call the 24/7 dispatch line.

What separates this from a general burst pipe is the cause and the timing. Ice-damaged lines are usually in unheated places such as exterior walls, garages, crawl spaces and basements near rim joists. The damage often appears during a thaw, sometimes while the family is out, so the water can run for hours before anyone knows.

When a thawed pipe is an emergency

Treat any water coming from a once-frozen pipe as urgent. If a line has been frozen but is not leaking yet, the safest assumption is that it may crack when it thaws. After-hours calls are normal in cold weather, and same-day response is offered when crews are available.

  • Close the main valve before you try to thaw anything.
  • Never use an open flame on a pipe. Use a hair dryer or warm towels only if the line is not already leaking.
  • Open cabinet doors under sinks on exterior walls to let room heat reach the lines.
  • If you see any wet drywall or ceiling stain, stop thawing and call for help.
  • Photograph frost, splits and wet areas before the pipe is repaired.

Michigan winters and why pipes fail

Southeast Michigan winters bring below-freezing stretches followed by thaws, which is exactly the cycle that cracks stressed pipes. A line that froze and thawed once may be weakened for the next freeze.

Pipes in garages, along exterior walls and in crawl spaces are the usual victims, and homes with additions or poorly insulated rim areas are exposed at those spots. Ask your water utility or a licensed plumber about insulating exposed lines before the next cold snap, and keep the thermostat on in any room with plumbing even when you travel.

If you want numbers for your own address, the National Weather Service climate pages list freeze-day normals for the Detroit area.

How a frozen pipe cleanup is done

The crew extracts water first, as described on the water extraction page, then opens the wet cavity. Frozen lines often split inside walls, so removal of baseboards and a flood cut in the drywall exposes the damaged section for the plumber. Insulation that is wet is replaced because it holds water against the framing.

Drying then follows the same plan as any supply-line loss, using air movers, dehumidifiers and daily readings. Rooms below the break usually need attention too, since water travels down. See ceiling water damage repair when the water came through a ceiling.

Questions people ask

Do you handle frozen pipe emergencies at night or on weekends in Taylor?

Yes. The dispatch line is open 24/7. Thaw-related leaks often start overnight, so after-hours calls are routine, with same-day response when crews are available.

How do I know a pipe froze without leaking?

Look for no water or a trickle from a faucet, frost on exposed lines or a cold, bulging section. Treat it as cracked until a plumber says otherwise.

Is it safe to thaw a pipe myself?

Only slowly and only if there is no leak. Use a hair dryer or warm towels, never an open flame, and keep the main valve closed if you suspect a crack.

Will insurance cover a frozen pipe?

Often, when the break was sudden and the heat was on. Policies differ on vacancy and heating requirements, so ask your insurer.

Can the pipe be fixed without opening the wall?

Sometimes, but a hidden split usually requires opening drywall to repair and to dry the cavity. A plumber and the restoration crew can coordinate.
