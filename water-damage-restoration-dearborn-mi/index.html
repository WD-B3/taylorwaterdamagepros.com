Dearborn neighborhoods and the houses in them

Dearborn is the largest of the five towns on this site, with 109,976 residents in 2020 on 24.51 square miles. It developed along both sides of the Rouge River, where the Upper, Middle and Lower branches meet. Henry Ford shaped much of what you see: the Ford River Rouge Complex, the Henry Ford Museum and Greenfield Village that opened in 1929 and the Fair Lane estate.

Edsel Ford established the Historic Springwells Park Neighborhood in 1939 to give company executives and auto workers better housing, and the Ford Homes Historic District is also on the books. Houses from that era often have plaster walls, brick or wood framing and original interior trim.

The practical consequence for water damage is that matching old trim and plaster costs more than hanging new drywall, and any work in a historic district may need city approval, so ask the city before you start.

When water is spreading in Dearborn

Water moves through older construction in ways newer homes do not. In a plaster-and-lath wall, water can run down behind the plaster before it shows, and the wall may need opening in a way that keeps as much original material as possible. The wall and drywall page explains how moisture is mapped and where cuts go, and the structural drying page explains how the cavity is dried.

Call the line, shut off the main and keep power off in wet areas where it is safe. Night calls from Dearborn are routine, and a crew goes out the same day when one is available.

Before we arrive

Stop the water

Close the main water valve or the supply to the failed fixture.

Panel and outlets

If water is near them, stay out. Call the utility or 911 before calling us.

Document

Photograph walls, floors and belongings. Keep samples of trim and finishes for matching.

Keep clear

Avoid dirty water and keep children and pets away from it.

Streets, the river and the place you are standing

Warren Avenue is the commercial center of Dearborn's Arab-American community, the South End sits near Morningside Street, and Springwells was once a separate community before it was absorbed. Michigan Avenue and the Rouge River meet at the site where Conrad Ten Eyck built a tavern in 1826, and Henry Ford was born at Ford Road and Greenfield Road. Dispatchers use roads like these to place a call, so give them a cross street.

The river corridor matters for flood questions. Check your address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center if you live near the Rouge, and ask the city about local rules.

The 2014 storm hit the neighboring community of Dearborn Heights hard, where Ecorse Creek reached a record stage, and the flood damage page explains what that kind of water means for the cleanup. Commercial buildings, including those near the Fairlane Town Center that opened in 1976, follow the commercial page.

Cost factors in Dearborn

Price follows the water category, the area, the materials, the drying days and the rebuild. In an older home, matching plaster, trim and flooring is often the biggest variable. Scope and price are explained before work starts.

Tell your insurer you have water damage, take photos or video and keep damaged items until your insurer says you can discard them. Sewer backup and sump pump failures may be covered, and flood damage is not covered by a standard policy. Crews also work in Garden City and Inkster, and in Taylor at 48180.

Questions people ask

Do you cover every ZIP code in Dearborn?

Yes. We cover 48120 and 48126 across the central and eastern parts of the city, where the Rouge River branches meet and the Ford Rouge Complex and Springwells areas sit, and 48124 and 48128 across the west side toward the Taylor direction. The ZIPs 48121 and 48123 are PO box ZIPs.

Is emergency water removal available in Dearborn?

Yes. The dispatch line is open 24 hours a day, every day, and a crew can be sent from Taylor to Dearborn when one is available.

Do I need approval to repair a house in a historic district?

Possibly. Ask the City of Dearborn before changing exterior or protected features. Interior drying and removal of wet materials is a separate question.

How do you repair old plaster walls?

Wet plaster is dried where it can be and removed where it cannot. The scope explains what is replaced with drywall and what is matched.

Is Dearborn in the same service area as Dearborn Heights?

Dearborn Heights is a separate city. Call the line and ask whether a crew can reach you.
