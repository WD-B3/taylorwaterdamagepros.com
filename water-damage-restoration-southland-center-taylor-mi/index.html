Water Damage Restoration Near Southland Center, Taylor, MI

Retail, office and nearby residential water cleanup along the Eureka Road shopping district.

☎ (734) 865-2522

Eureka Road is the shopping and business spine of Taylor. It runs east and west through the city and meets I-75. This page is for businesses and nearby homes around the Southland Center area at Eureka and Pardee, described by landmarks, because no source we found sets an official boundary for the district.

Landmarks that locate you

Southland Center, a regional mall, opened in 1970 at Eureka and Pardee Roads. At the eastern end, Trader's Pointe opened in June 2018 on the former Gibraltar Trade Center site at Eureka Road and I-75. The 1882 West Mound church once stood on Eureka Road, across from the mall, before it was moved to Heritage Park.

Our listed address is also on Eureka Road. That is a fact about where the business is listed and not a claim that every call here is near. Give dispatch the exact building, and the crew plan will follow.

Why shops and offices need a different plan

A water loss in a store or office is a business problem as well as a building problem. Doors may need to stay open, stock may be wet, and a shared wall can carry water into the unit next door. The first questions are which areas can stay in use and which must close. The commercial water damage page explains how a scope can be staged to limit downtime.

Tenants, landlords and records

In a leased unit, tell the property manager at once, because the owner controls the building systems and often the insurance. Record the time you found the water and where it came from. Take photos of the damage and the source from a safe spot. Keep receipts for any stock or equipment moved, since your insurer may ask for them.

Coverage depends on the cause and the policy, and only your insurer decides. Michigan's regulator advises telling your insurer you have water damage and keeping damaged items until it says they can be discarded. A business policy can differ from a homeowners policy, so check both the building and the contents terms.

Homes near the corridor

Houses around Eureka Road face the same residential problems as the rest of the city, including supply lines, water heaters and basement water after heavy rain. On August 11, 2014, Taylor received more than 4 inches of rain in a day, and nearly 200 residents contacted the city about damage. The reports do not name streets near Eureka Road, so we do not claim this area was affected.

Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status not verified. Look up an address on FEMA's Flood Map Service Center before you decide how much flood cover to buy. The water extraction page explains the first stage of any cleanup.

If you are on the Southgate side

Eureka Road continues west into Southgate, where the Southgate Shopping Center opened in 1957 at Eureka and Trenton. If your address is across that line, see the Southgate page for the local details.

What to do while you wait

  • Stay out of water near outlets, panels and equipment.
  • Shut off the water supply only if the valve is dry and reachable.
  • Keep staff and customers away from standing or contaminated water.
  • Photograph the damage before anything is moved.
  • Call and describe whether the damage is in one unit or several.

Questions people ask

Do you handle water damage in stores and offices near Southland Center?

Yes. Commercial leaks are handled by the same dispatch line. Say whether the building is shared and whether the space must stay open, so the plan can be staged.

Is the Eureka Road shopping district in a FEMA flood zone?

Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status not verified. Check the exact address on FEMA's Flood Map Service Center, and remember that Zone X does not rule out water entering.

Who pays for water damage in a leased unit?

It depends on the lease and on which policy covers the building and the contents. Tell the property manager and your insurer, and ask each what applies.

Can a leak in one unit damage the neighboring unit?

Yes. Water can follow framing, ceilings and shared walls, which is why moisture readings should extend beyond the visible wet area.

Do you also help homes along Eureka Road?

Yes. Homes in the area get the same extraction and drying service, with the same first steps described on this page.
