About Taylor Water Damage Pros

A local dispatch line and restoration crews for Taylor, Michigan and the towns around it.

☎ (734) 865-2522

Taylor Water Damage Pros is a water damage restoration service based at 23351 Eureka Road in Taylor, MI. We take calls at all hours for burst pipes, failed water heaters, sewer backups, storm water and every other kind of unwanted water in a house or a building. The number is (734) 865-2522 and it is open 24 hours a day.

What we do

Our work follows the order water damage demands. Stop the source, remove standing water, dry what is wet, clean what the water touched and repair what cannot be saved. The first three steps are time sensitive, so the line is staffed around the clock. The last two can wait for a plan that you and your insurer understand.

  • Emergency water removal and extraction.
  • Structural drying with air movers and dehumidifiers.
  • Cleanup after pipe, appliance, roof and storm failures.
  • Sewage and contaminated water cleanup with protective gear.
  • Repairs to drywall, flooring and finishes after drying.

The full list is on the services page. Each service has its own page that explains the problem, the method and the questions we hear most.

How we work with you

We explain the scope and the price before work starts. That means you hear what will be removed, what will be dried, which equipment goes in and what the likely next steps are, in plain terms and before anyone starts. We document the loss with photos and moisture readings, which your insurer may ask for. We do not promise claim approval, because only your insurer decides coverage.

Where we work

We serve Taylor first, then nearby towns including Garden City, Riverview, Southgate, Inkster and Dearborn. The service area page lists every town and ZIP code we cover.

Licensed and insured

Our crews are licensed and insured. If you want to confirm a contractor's state license for any job, the Michigan Department of Licensing and Regulatory Affairs keeps a public License Information database. Ask us any question about scope, price or paperwork and we will answer it before you decide.

What a first call looks like

You describe the problem and give the address. The dispatcher asks whether the water is still running, where the shutoff is and whether anyone is in danger. If the answer is yes to danger, the first instruction is to leave and call 911 or the utility. If not, you get steps to slow the damage while a crew is sent.

Once the crew is on site, they find the source if it is not stopped, take moisture readings and explain the plan. You hear what will be removed, what will be dried and what the price covers before the equipment goes in. If you want your insurer on the line, the crew will document the loss so you have the records.

How we write what you read here

The guides on this site cite public sources such as the Michigan insurance regulator, the EPA, FEMA, the National Archives and the National Weather Service. Each guide has a local section with Taylor facts drawn from the Census and other public records. We do not publish reviews, ratings or awards on this site, because we only show what we can verify.

If you find something that looks wrong, write to us. Water damage advice depends on facts, and facts change. We would rather fix a page than leave an error up.

Why a local base matters

Water damage is a race against time, and drive time is part of the race. A base on Eureka Road puts crews near I-94, I-75 and Telegraph Road. That is why we focus on Taylor and the towns around it instead of trying to cover the whole state.

What we will not do

We will not promise that your insurer pays, because only the insurer decides. We will not quote a firm price over the phone for work nobody has seen. We will not tell you something is dangerous to sell you a bigger job. If a small dry-out is all you need, we will say so, and we will tell you how to do it yourself.

We also will not hide the limits of our work. A crew dries and cleans. A plumber fixes pipes, an electrician checks wiring and a roofer fixes a roof. When your problem needs one of them, we will say so and explain what to ask.

Call us

If you have water in the house now, call (734) 865-2522. If you are planning ahead, you can also reach us at contact@taylorwaterdamagepros.com.
