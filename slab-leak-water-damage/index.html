Emergency Slab Leak Water Damage in Taylor, MI

A leak in a line under the concrete is hard to see and easy to miss. Warm floors and a spinning water meter are the clues. Dispatch is 24/7.

☎ (734) 865-2522

Slab leak water damage in Taylor, MI comes from a pressurized water line that runs through or under a concrete slab.

Most slab leaks are found by symptom, not by sight: a water bill that jumps, a meter that keeps spinning with all fixtures off, a warm spot on the floor or the sound of running water when nothing is on. If you suspect one, shut the main valve and call our 24/7 dispatch line for the damage while a plumber locates the break.

This page is written around the mechanism, not a claim that slab leaks are common here. Many Michigan homes have basements, which have slab floors too, and some additions, garages and commercial units sit on slabs with lines in or under the concrete. Where your home has pipes in the floor, this page applies. The burst pipe page covers a visible line instead.

When a slab leak is an emergency

A leak that you can hear, see or feel in the floor is urgent. Water under a slab lifts flooring, soaks the pad and the subfloor and can undermine soil beneath the concrete. After-hours calls are routine, and same-day response is offered when crews are available. Shut the water off at the main and keep using the house only if the plumber confirms it is safe.

  • Close the main valve and check whether the water meter stops.
  • Look for warm or wet spots on the floor and for new cracks along baseboards.
  • Photograph damp flooring, stained baseboards and the meter reading.
  • Call a leak-detection plumber. Pinpointing the leak is their job.
  • Call us to extract, dry and repair once the line is shut off.

Signs that point under the floor

SignWhat it suggests
Warm spot on the floorA hot water line leaking under the slab
Meter runs with everything offA leak somewhere after the meter
Damp or lifting flooringWater pushing up through the slab or its joints
Musty smell with no visible sourceMoisture trapped under flooring
Sound of running waterA pressurized line leaking

Because the leak is under concrete, the cause is usually corrosion, a poor joint, abrasion from soil or settling. The plumber repairs by breaking the slab and replacing the section, rerouting the line above the floor or relining. That is a plumbing decision, and our work is the water damage around it.

Drying a slab and the floor above it

Concrete holds moisture and releases it slowly, so slab drying takes longer than drywall drying. The crew uses moisture meters on the slab, removes soaked pad and flooring and sets air movers and dehumidifiers. Desiccant units can help when the slab is slow to release moisture. The process is shown on the structural drying page.

Finished flooring on top matters. Hardwood and laminate over a wet slab can cup or buckle, which is a repair covered on the hardwood floor page. Carpet and pad usually come up and are replaced, as the carpet page explains.

Slab leak cost factors

Price follows the area of slab and flooring affected, the number of drying days, the materials above the slab and the rebuild. The plumbing repair is separate and can be a large item. Scope and price for the restoration are explained before work starts.

Michigan regulators say sudden and accidental water damage is often covered by a homeowners policy, while long-term seepage is often excluded. Slab leaks are tricky for claims because they can run for a while before discovery. Insurers differ on how they treat the cost of reaching the pipe, so read your policy and ask your insurer. Questions people ask

Do you handle slab leak emergencies at night or on weekends in Taylor?

Yes. The dispatch line is open 24 hours a day, every day. If a slab leak is wetting your floor, call at once, and same-day response is offered when crews are available.

Does a Michigan home with a basement have slab leaks?

It can. Basement floors are concrete slabs, and some homes have lines running in or under them. Garages and additions are other places lines run through concrete.

How is a slab leak found?

A plumber uses listening devices, pressure tests and sometimes thermal imaging. You should not break the concrete yourself.

Will insurance cover a slab leak?

It depends on the policy and on whether the water damage was sudden. Some policies exclude the cost of reaching the pipe. Ask your insurer exactly what applies.

How long does a slab take to dry?

Concrete releases moisture slowly. The crew uses readings to decide, and the timeline can be longer than a drywall dry-out.
